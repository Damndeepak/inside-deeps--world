What the app collects

When you continue with Google or Guest, Deeplist stores your profile, account identifier, basic app-open/last-active information, app version and approximate city/country derived from your internet connection. The app admin can see these records and block or unblock accounts. Guest names are user-chosen or generated, not verified identities. Google sign-in provides your Google name, email and photo.

What stays private

Listening history, saved lyric moments and local playlists are not uploaded by this profile system. Only playlists and favourite songs you explicitly choose to publish are public. Your approximate location and account activity are never on your public profile. No GPS permission, contacts or private files are collected for analytics. Optional website live-sharing has its own controls.

Accuracy and access

City/country estimates may be wrong, especially with VPNs and mobile networks. Blocks apply when the official app connects to Deeplist services; offline devices cannot receive a new block immediately. Guests can create a new identity after clearing app data. Public music sources are not controlled by Deeplist.

Retention and deletion

Basic account data is retained until you delete your account from Profile. Sessions expire after 30 days; one-use sign-in codes expire in 2 minutes. Access-control audit records are retained for security and contain account IDs and timestamps. To request help or deletion, contact gandhideepak6376@gmail.com. Deleting an account does not remove local downloads or data from your phone.
