What the app collects

When you continue with Google or Guest, Deeplist stores your profile, account identifier, basic app-open/last-active information, app version and approximate city/country derived from your internet connection. The app admin can see these records and block or unblock accounts. Guest names are user-chosen or generated, not verified identities. Google sign-in provides your Google name, email and photo.

What stays private

When you connect Google, Deeplist automatically backs up and syncs your playlists, likes, listening history, searches, saved lyric moments and appearance settings in private app cloud storage associated with your account. This is not your Gmail inbox. Sync runs while the app is open or playing music; Profile also offers Sync now. The current snapshot limit is 15 MB. Audio files, downloads, local recordings and integration login credentials stay on your device. Guests keep this data locally until they connect Google. Cloud snapshots require the matching signed-in account and are not shown on public profiles or the usage admin dashboard. Only playlists and favourite songs you explicitly choose to publish are public. Your approximate location and account activity are never on your public profile. No GPS permission, contacts or private files are collected for analytics. Optional website live-sharing has its own controls.

Listen Together requests

A website visitor can send their website nickname and a request to your paired app. Requests expire after two minutes. You can accept, reject or pause that visitor’s requests for five minutes. App requests are checked while the app is open or music is playing. Accepting starts a social listening flow; website live-sharing remains public under its existing controls, so rejecting a request does not make an already public player private.

Accuracy and access

City/country estimates may be wrong, especially with VPNs and mobile networks. Blocks apply when the official app connects to Deeplist services; offline devices cannot receive a new block immediately. Guests can create a new identity after clearing app data. Public music sources are not controlled by Deeplist.

Retention and deletion

Basic account data and the current private cloud backup are retained until you delete your account from Profile. A previous cloud snapshot may be retained temporarily to handle concurrent sync reads. Account deletion revokes sessions and removes cloud backups; temporary storage deletion failures are retried by hourly cleanup. Sessions expire after 30 days; one-use sign-in codes expire in 2 minutes. Access-control audit records are retained for security and contain account IDs and timestamps.
