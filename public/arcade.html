TUTORIAL // 01

how to survive

1. DRAG • touch and drag anywhere inside the game to move the node.
2. DODGE • avoid the falling outlined blocks. You only lose when your node actually touches one.
3. SURVIVE • your score rises with time, and the world gets faster.
4. GLITCHES • watch the warning text. Some events can temporarily flip your controls.
5. RECORD • your first game asks for your real name. Later scores save automatically under that name.
